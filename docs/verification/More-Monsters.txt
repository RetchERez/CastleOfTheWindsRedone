Castle of the Winds I & II: Redone
More Monsters v0.1 Preview and All-in-One v1.2 Preview

Native spawning

The Centipede and Beetle were added as two independent creature types in
each game. The allocator, terrain checks, population budgets, and ordinary
spawn positions remain native. Tested fresh procedural generation, active
timed respawns and off-level return repopulation. Deterministic witnesses
covered all 16 allowed floor/species combinations across both chapters.
The protected floors, scripted boss placement, no-tile case, population
cap and one-live-per-species cap all passed. Native clone still works.
Changing creature construction can change later random results inside an
existing spawn budget, such as the stock optional second timed spawn.

Combat and persistence

Native attack tests exercised the Centipede's 1d4 physical bite and
weaker native poison and the Beetle's 1d6 physical bite. Both monster
types resolved to their own original 32×32 icon and their full names.
All 1,079 preexisting native strings matched the original in each game.

Four native generation seeds produced positive copper drops. The actors
and their copper were moved into repeatable adjacent test encounters,
then opened in Windows 3.1 under a private DOSBox display. In both
chapters and both builds, each actor kept its type, HP and attacks after
saving and reopening. The player killed it, received the correct XP,
collected its copper with Get, and kept the copper after saving and
reopening again. Loot was native copper and varied with dungeon depth.

Part One Giant Centipede — 4 XP, 152 copper in the tested encounter
Part One Giant Beetle — 6 XP, 176 copper in the tested encounter
Part Two Giant Centipede — 4 XP, 1043 copper in the tested encounter
Part Two Giant Beetle — 6 XP, 1200 copper in the tested encounter

The new designs are tuned against the original Viper and Giant Red Ant.
The Centipede has lower HP, lower direct damage and lower poison
magnitude than the Viper. The Beetle has higher HP and defense than the
Giant Red Ant, with lower damage and a slower turn rate. New monsters
replace candidates in a similar ordinary XP range and appear only on
the listed floors.

Stacked regression

The combined executables passed the same spawn and combat checkpoints.
The DiabloLoot native rewards and shop stocking checks passed as well.
