Castle of the Winds I & II: Redone
More Monsters v1.0 and All-in-One v1.0 verification

Spawning and game rules

The original Win16 procedural picker produced all 25 new Part I types and
all 40 new Part II types in both editions, within their configured floor
ranges. Every new type passed the one-live-per-species ordinary spawn
check and the original Clone Monster spell test. Native active timer and
off-level repopulation were exercised for all 8 Part I and 9 Part II
combat roles.

In 105 matched native map generations per edition, total populations
and ordinary picker call counts matched the original games. All 93
scripted actor placement calls kept their original types and positions.
New actors stayed outside the recorded scripted rooms. Castle 4 in Part
II kept its original population while the prisoner rescue was pending.
Different creature construction can change a later random roll within
the original spawn budget.

The published profiles use original 32×32 color and monochrome sprites
and balance their stats against native creatures. Native tests verified
every new attack slot, elemental resistances, the caster limit and melee
fallback, Dark Sorcerer Slow and expiry, regeneration timing and HP caps,
and Black Slime's half physical damage and ordinary fire damage. All
1,079 existing game strings matched the originals in both chapters.

Windows 3.1 gameplay in headless DOSBox

All 65 chapter/species encounters passed in All-in-One, alongside 8
representative encounters in standalone More Monsters. Controlled fixtures
used creatures and copper generated by the original engine with the mod
installed. Each actor was saved and reloaded alive. The player killed it,
earned exactly the expected XP, collected the drop, saved, and reloaded
again. The collected copper and gear survived the final reload. These
controlled encounters use increased player damage. Natural spawning was
verified separately using the unaltered placement routines.

All-in-One regressions

Native combat and shop tests verified the shared enhancement routine,
initial shop stock, restocks, and the same weapon and armor tiers in
both games. The controlled combat sweep produced 3 randomized gear drops
in Part I and 25 in Part II. Part II drops included Enchanted and Rare
items. Eight additional Part I Black Slime encounters produced five
randomized drops, including Enchanted Leather Armor and an Enchanted Keen
Dagger. Both were verified on the floor, after pickup and after reload.
Controlled native kills at deeper levels exercised all tiers in
both chapters, including Legendary and concealed cursed gear. Live curse
checks in both chapters verified first-equip reveal, refused removal,
applied stat penalties, and persistence after reload.

Matched map captures changed 18,432 Part I pixels and 65,762 Part II
pixels from white to black. Every changed pixel was in the map pane.
The final art update changed only the two Black Slime icon resources in
each executable. The new icons use black and white with a transparent
mask and were checked through the actual game in both chapters.

Distribution

All 26 supported source/installer combinations produced the expected
chapter binaries and original help files. Windows Setup ran from its
bundled runtime without Python on PATH. Invalid ZIP and existing
destination checks also passed. The portable archives contain patches,
installer files and verification images.
