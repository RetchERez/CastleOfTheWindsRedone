Castle of the Winds Redone
DiabloLoot v1.1 verification

KILLS, PICKUP, AND RELOAD

Both standalone DiabloLoot games ran in Windows 3.1 under DOSBox on a
private Linux display. A live Goblin Fighter kill in each chapter
awarded 6 XP, put new randomized gear alongside the native copper,
and allowed pickup through Get. The original tile emptied. Fresh
loads preserved the item's type, stats, rarity tag, and reveal state.

Part One dropped leather armor with the native cursed condition and
a hidden Enchanted tier. It kept its native -5 armor modifier and
zero value. The hidden tier was still present after pickup and load.

Part Two dropped an Enchanted Keen Broad Sword with +1 to hit. Its
name, value, and active stat modifier survived pickup and load.

Controlled tests also ran the shipped game code at the native damage,
item factory, shop generation, restock, and save/load entry points.
Both games produced Common, Enchanted, Rare, and Legendary weapons
and body armor on enemy death at a selected deeper dungeon level.
The tests exercised both initial stock and restocks in two shops per
game and verified the same enhancement routine on kills and shop
rolls. They also exercised native reward preservation in no-drop and
nonlethal cases. No seed controls are present in the patch.

CURSES AND RARITY COLORS

Both games kept a cursed item's rarity text and icon accent hidden
before first equip, including after native Identify. An incompatible
equip attempt kept the tier hidden. Successful equip revealed it.
The tier survived an ordinary save and reopen and the Remove Curse
spell. The native curse stopped both the weapon and armor from being
moved out of their slots. Once Remove Curse freed them, unequipping
removed their active stat effects. Clean-game controls and the mod
applied the same curse effects in the tested cases.

The included curse captures use the stacked builds. Both showed
blue Enchanted, yellow Rare, and both true orange shades of the
Legendary icon accent in the actual 32-bit Win16 game display.
Indexed displays render the Legendary accent as red-and-yellow.
