Castle of the Winds Redone
DiabloLoot v1.2

The new rarity brackets leave the native sprite and native curse markings
intact. The variant generator now adds wider accents on all four corners
and along the bottom edge. Equipment names still carry the tier.

The resource tests checked every decorated weapon and body-armor icon
in both chapters. Headless Windows 3.1 inventory tests ran both chapters
in indexed and 32-bit true-color modes, using the All-in-One v1.1 builds.
Revealed orange pixels on the equipped Legendary Chain Mail increased
from 6 to 32 in each chapter. The tested equipped Rare War Hammer had
232 yellow pixels, up from 56. The pack showed distinct blue, yellow
and orange accents on all three examples. Indexed mode showed a red
and yellow pattern for Legendary. The cursed controls hid those accents
prior to equipping and kept their reveal state and native curse through
save and reload.
